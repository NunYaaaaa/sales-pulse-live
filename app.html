ℹ️ How the totals work: Gross is your sales after refunds, without the sales tax and retail delivery fees Etsy collects for tax authorities. Fees are everything Etsy charged (transaction, processing, listing, ads, shipping labels) minus the fee credits it gave back. Payouts, bounced payouts and card top-ups only move money between your bank and your Etsy balance, so they're left out of both. Net = Gross − Fees: the change in your balance apart from those transfers.
