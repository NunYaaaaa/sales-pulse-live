How It Works

Three steps to your numbers.

Sales Pulse Live connects through your own Etsy developer app, so the access Etsy grants is yours alone, and read-only.

01
🔑

Create an Etsy App

Register an app at etsy.com/developers and add https://nunyaaaaa.github.io/sales-pulse-live/app.html as its redirect URI. Etsy gives you a keystring and a shared secret.

02
🔐

Connect Your Shop

Paste the keystring and shared secret, then approve read-only access on Etsy's own sign-in page. Your Etsy password never reaches Sales Pulse Live.

03
📊

Explore & Export

Pick a date range, explore the charts, orders, fee breakdown and insights, then download your orders or ledger as CSV or JSON.

Privacy & Data
Read Only. Always.
✓  Built on the Etsy Open API v3
✓
Read-only access

Sales Pulse Live asks Etsy only for read permissions. It can't edit listings, orders or anything else in your shop.

✓
OAuth 2.0 + PKCE

You sign in on Etsy's own page, so your Etsy password never reaches Sales Pulse Live. Your API keys and access token stay in this browser tab's session storage and are cleared when you disconnect or close the tab.

✓
Nothing saved on a server

Requests pass through a small proxy that lets your browser talk to Etsy. Your orders and ledger are held in the page's memory only and aren't saved anywhere.

✓
Gentle on Etsy's API

Requests are paced and back off automatically when Etsy rate-limits them, and recent results are reused instead of fetched again. Listings and reviews are only requested when you open Insights.
